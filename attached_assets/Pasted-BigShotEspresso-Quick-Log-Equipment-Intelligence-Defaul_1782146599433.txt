BigShotEspresso – Quick Log, Equipment Intelligence, Defaults Inheritance, and User Experience Refinement

Goal:

Continue evolving BigShotEspresso into a platform focused on:

Log. Analyze. Repeat.

Do not redesign the application.
Do not rebuild the database.
Do not change Airtable sync architecture.
Do not implement subscriptions, billing, auth, or AI Coach yet.

Focus on:

- Quick Log
- Logging Preferences
- Equipment Intelligence
- Defaults Inheritance
- Beginner Simplicity
- Advanced User Flexibility

--------------------------------------------------
CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE
--------------------------------------------------

A shot should take less than 10 seconds to record.

The app should adapt to the user.

Beginners should see only a few fields.

Advanced users should be able to track a complete espresso system.

--------------------------------------------------
1. QUICK LOG FOUNDATION
--------------------------------------------------

Create a Quick Log system.

Quick Log should only display fields enabled in Logging Preferences.

Keep existing advanced/full logging.

Quick Log is an additional workflow.

--------------------------------------------------
2. LOGGING PREFERENCES
--------------------------------------------------

Organize preferences into groups.

A. Essential
Enabled by default:

- Dose
- Yield
- Shot Time
- Rating

B. Timing

Optional:

- First Pour Delay
- Pour Time
- Scale Time

Important:

Pour Time and Scale Time are separate fields.

C. Brew Setup

Optional:

- Temperature
- Grind Setting
- Grinder Time
- Grinder Output Weight
- Grind Changed This Shot

D. Classification

Optional:

- Reference Shot
- Signature Shot
- Sour Shot
- Shot Status
- Shot Classification
- Notes

--------------------------------------------------
3. RATING MODES
--------------------------------------------------

Add setting:

Rating Input Mode

Options:

Easy Rating

- 1 to 10 stars
- Beginner friendly

Precision Rating

- Numeric
- 0.00 to 10.00
- Two decimal places

Examples:

7.75
8.25
9.50

Default:

Easy Rating

Store internally as numeric values.

--------------------------------------------------
12. GRIND CHANGED CHECKBOX
--------------------------------------------------

When Grind Setting is enabled:

Show:

Grind Changed This Shot

Purpose:

Quickly identify grind adjustments.

Supports:

- Dial-in analysis
- Grind drift analysis
- Future DIM calculations

--------------------------------------------------
13. GRINDER OUTPUT SEPARATION
--------------------------------------------------

Keep separate:

Dose

Actual coffee used in basket.

Grinder Output Weight

Coffee produced by grinder before trimming, overflow, or spillage.

Grinder Time

Time grinder operated.

Do not treat these as the same measurement.

--------------------------------------------------
16. PRESERVE BEGINNER EXPERIENCE
--------------------------------------------------

Do not force users to configure equipment.

Quick Log should still work with:

- Dose
- Yield
- Shot Time
- Rating

No equipment setup required.

--------------------------------------------------
17. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------

BigShotEspresso exists to help users:

Log.
Analyze.
Repeat.

The goal is not to find one perfect shot.

The goal is to discover repeatable success.

All future intelligence should support this philosophy.
