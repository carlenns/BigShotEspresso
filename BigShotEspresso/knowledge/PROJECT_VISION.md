# BigShotEspresso

## Tagline
Find your best shot. Repeat it.

## Vision
BigShotEspresso exists to help home baristas make the coffee they actually want to drink.

Most coffee apps record what happened.
BigShotEspresso helps users understand why it happened, what worked, and how to reproduce it.

## Core Philosophy
BigShotEspresso is a coffee improvement platform.

Every feature should help users:
- Dial in faster
- Waste less coffee
- Reproduce successful shots
- Understand preferences
- Discover patterns

## The BigShotEspresso Test
Does this help the user find their best shot and repeat it?

## Company Mission
Help home baristas make the coffee they actually want to drink.
