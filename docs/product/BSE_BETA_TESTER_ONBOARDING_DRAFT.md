## What it does today

- **Log a shot** with your dose, grind, timing, yield, rating and notes. Tap **Log Shot**.
- **Track bags and beans** so each shot belongs to the coffee you were drinking. See **Bags** and **Beans**.
- **Look back** in **Shot Log**, and flag your best shots as **Reference Shots**.
- **See patterns** on the **Dashboard** for your current bag, based only on your own shots.
- **Tag taste** with the selector chips (you can hide ones you don't use).

On a phone, swipe the bar along the bottom to reach every page.

## Your first ten minutes

1. Sign in with the invitation we sent you. **[TBD: sign-in steps once login exists]**
2. Add the coffee you're drinking in **Beans**, then open it as a bag in **Bags**.
3. Pull a shot and tap **Log Shot**. Only a few fields matter at first; the form pre-fills sensible defaults.
4. Open **Dashboard** after a handful of shots to see your first patterns.

## What to expect (the honest list)

- **It's a beta.** You may hit bugs or confusing screens. That's exactly what we want to hear about.
- **It can be slow to wake.** The first load after a quiet spell may take a few seconds.
- **Backups are weekly.** Keep your own notes for anything you can't afford to lose for a week.
- **Your data is yours and private to you.** Other testers can't see it. We don't sell it or use it to train AI. See the
  [Privacy Policy](BSE_PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md) and [Beta Terms](BSE_TERMS_OF_SERVICE_DRAFT.md).
- **Not available to testers yet:** CSV import, Airtable connection, sharing with other users, and any billing.
