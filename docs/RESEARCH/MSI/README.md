# Model Exception Intelligence

## Research question

Why do mechanical predictions and sensory outcomes disagree?

## Scope

- Model Flag and agreement
- Taste Zone versus predicted Zone
- Boundary Shots
- Important-to-Intelligence records
- Flow diagnostics and hidden-variable evidence

## Principle

Mechanical explanations take precedence over operator blame. Exceptions are evidence for refining the model, not records to discard.

## Status

**Conceptual/evidence-ready, not implementation-ready.** Exception taxonomy and confidence policy require approval.
