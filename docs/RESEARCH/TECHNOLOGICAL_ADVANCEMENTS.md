# Technological Advancements

> **Status:** Register opened 2026-08-29; no advancement claimed.

## Purpose

This register records demonstrated changes in technological capability or knowledge supported by completed experiments. It is not a marketing-claims page and does not by itself establish SR&ED eligibility.

## Advancement rule

Do not create a substantive advancement claim until evidence identifies:

- the previous capability and its limitation;
- the uncertainty addressed;
- the experiments and raw evidence;
- the knowledge gained;
- the demonstrated new capability, if any;
- remaining uncertainty and limits of generalization.

Negative knowledge may be important, but it must state exactly what was ruled out and under what conditions.
