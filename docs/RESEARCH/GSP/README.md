# Grind Success Predictor

## Research question

Which grinder settings and operating conditions are most likely to produce a successful espresso result?

## Scope

- Grinder setting and time
- Dose, Yield, Flow Time, and Pour Delay
- Bag lifecycle
- Hopper context
- Historical reference evidence
- Confidence-bounded starting-point recommendations

## Boundary

GSP cannot fabricate a universal setting. Predictions must be scoped to equipment, coffee, Bag, evidence, and confidence.

## Status

**Conceptual/evidence-ready, not implementation-ready.** It depends on stable DCI, OSI, HMI, BLI, and MSI foundations.
