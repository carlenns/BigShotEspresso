# Failed and Inconclusive Approaches

> **Status:** Register opened 2026-08-29; no historical failure reconstructed by this task.

## Purpose

Preserve experimental approaches that failed, behaved unexpectedly, produced contradictory evidence, or could not be evaluated. Failure records prevent repeated work and support an honest account of how knowledge changed.

Routine defects and ordinary implementation mistakes belong in normal development records unless they occurred within and materially informed a documented experiment.
